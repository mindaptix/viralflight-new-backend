Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Applies to the Viral Flight mobile app and related services at viralflight.cloud / viralflight.in

Viral Flight (“we”, “us”, “our”) helps creators, brands, and agencies discover collaborations and manage campaigns. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: mobile number (OTP login), role (creator / brand / agency), and profile information you provide (name, city, bio, niches, languages, social handles, rates, media kit).
  • Optional manager contact: if you add a manager or agency contact, we store their name and WhatsApp number so business queries can be routed to them.
  • Profile media: photos you upload (for example, profile picture).
  • Usage data: app interactions such as campaign views, applications, connections, and basic device/app diagnostics needed to keep the service reliable.
  • Connected accounts: if you connect Instagram or other platforms, we process data returned by those platforms according to their permissions and policies.

2. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account (including OTP authentication).
  • Show your public creator/brand/agency profile to other users.
  • Power discovery, campaigns, applications, connections, and related product features.
  • Route collaboration queries to your manager contact when set.
  • Improve product quality, prevent abuse, and provide support.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing

We may share information with:

  • Other Viral Flight users as needed for the product (for example, brands viewing a creator profile you make visible).
  • Service providers that help us host, store, analyze, or communicate (such as cloud hosting, file storage, SMS/OTP providers), under appropriate safeguards.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. External apps & links

The app may open WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, email, or other external services. Those services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

5. Data retention

We keep account and profile data while your account is active. You may request correction or deletion of personal data subject to legal and operational requirements.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (including encrypted transport via HTTPS and access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children’s privacy

Viral Flight is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). Do not use the service if you do not meet the applicable age requirement.

8. Your choices

  • Edit profile details from the app (mobile number stays locked).
  • Add or remove a manager/agency WhatsApp contact.
  • Adjust contact preferences in settings where available (for example, whether to show personal mobile to brands).
  • Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion.

9. International users

We primarily operate for users in India. If you access Viral Flight from elsewhere, your information may be processed in India or other countries where our service providers operate.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
