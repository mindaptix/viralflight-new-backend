YouTube connection

Creators stay in control of their channel data.

When a creator chooses to connect YouTube, Viral Flight uses the authorized channel information to verify the connection and display relevant channel insights in the creator’s profile or media kit. Viral Flight does not publish videos or change a creator’s channel. Access can be revoked at any time through the creator’s Google Account. See our Privacy Policy for details.
